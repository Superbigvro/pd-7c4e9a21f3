What it does

  • DOCX files Open, edit, and save .docx documents.
  • Formatting Headings, lists, tables, images, and page setup.
  • PDF and print Export a fixed-layout copy or print from your PC.
  • Templates Start from a blank page or a ready-made structure.
  • Writing tools Spell check, focus mode, and text statistics.
  • Offline Everything works without an internet connection.

Local by design

PaperDesk is built to keep your writing on your computer.

  • Stays on your PC

    Documents are opened, edited, and saved locally.

  • No cloud

    There is no sync, no upload, and no account.

  • No telemetry

    PaperDesk does not send usage data.
